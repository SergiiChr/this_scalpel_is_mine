title: Bones
[b]Sawing[/b]: hold the saw on the bone and keep it there. Sawing skin cuts skin, badly.

[b]Dense bones[/b] stop a regular saw. Order the heavy saw (45 s) or use the mallet. The mallet works on anything but bruises everything around it.
[b]Fragile bones[/b] saw twice as fast and break if anything heavy lands on them. Don't drop the defibrillator on the patient.

[b]Setting a fracture[/b] takes two people.
1. Both grab a bone end with forceps.
2. Pull them back into line together.
3. Hold. Don't let go until the objective ticks.

[b]Broken ribs[/b] are set the same way: one person per broken end, pull them back into line and hold.
Splinters that broke off come out with a slow, steady pull. They bleed when they come free, so have suction ready.

[b]Nose job[/b]
The hump on the bridge of the nose is bone at the top and cartilage toward the tip, just under the skin.
Mark along the bridge, open it, then take the hump down with short taps of the mallet. Pick the loose piece out with forceps. Close with fine stitches: a face shows every mistake.

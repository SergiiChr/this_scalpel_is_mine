title: 16. Combined conditions
[b]16.1 Arterial surgery[/b]
Heparin [i]before[/i] clamping. Clamp before opening. Repair the vessel before releasing the clamp.

[b]16.2 Sympathomimetics[/b]
Cocaine and adrenaline together precipitate a cardiac event. After cocaine exposure, treat bradycardia with atropine and arrhythmia with amiodarone.

[b]16.3 Pacemaker, chest procedures[/b]
No electrocautery. No defibrillation unless the heart has arrested. Close with staples or sutures.

[b]16.4 Situs inversus[/b]
Plan the incision on the side opposite to the standard approach. Recorded on the chart as "dextrocardia".

[b]16.5 Heightened tactile sensitivity, conscious patient[/b]
Keep all contact within the operative field. Contact elsewhere causes sudden movement.

[b]16.6 Alcohol use with anesthetic resistance[/b]
Expect early and repeated lightening. Give propofol through the IV line at the first sign of speech.

[b]16.7 Prothrombotic tendency, long procedure[/b]
Anticoagulate early. Thrombosis can cause arrest without warning.

[b]16.8 Malignant hyperthermia risk, propofol unavailable[/b]
Use ketamine. Never the inhaled agent.

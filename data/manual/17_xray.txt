title: The X-ray cart
The portable X-ray lives in the corner of the OR. It shows what your eyes can't: bullets, blades, bone and anything you forgot inside.

1. Walk to the cart and grab the handle ([b]Interact[/b]). It follows you until you let go.
2. Park it by the table.
3. Take the X-ray. Stand back. It buzzes for a few seconds.
4. The print slides out of the front. It starts black and develops over about six seconds.

[table=2]
[cell][b]On the print[/b][/cell][cell][b]Means[/b][/cell]
[cell]Bright white[/cell][cell]Metal: bullets, knives, tools you dropped[/cell]
[cell]Light gray bar[/cell][cell]Bone[/cell]
[cell]Soft gray blob[/cell][cell]Masses, organs, clots[/cell]
[cell]Black pocket[/cell][cell]Trapped air[/cell]
[/table]

Take one before you close. A retained scalpel shows up on the next X-ray either way; better it's yours.
The cart needs half a minute between exposures.

title: Blood and transfusion
[img=520]res://assets/manual/diagrams/blood_types.svg[/img]

[b]O-negative[/b] is safe for almost everyone. Use it when you don't know the type.
The card lists a blood type, sometimes. The lab confirms it.

[b]Wrong blood[/b] causes fever, swelling, a pressure drop and a very bad week for the patient's kidneys.

[b]Bombay phenotype[/b]: incompatible with everything, O-negative included. Only the Bombay pack works. The nurse needs two minutes to find it. Order it the moment you see it on the card.

[b]Low blood count[/b] patients start with less. You have less time.

title: Anesthesia, deeper
[b]Depth[/b] shows as missing respiration on the monitor. A patient who starts talking is waking up.

[b]Resistant patients[/b] burn through anesthesia twice as fast. Keep a second dose ready and give it before they surface.
[b]Alcoholics[/b] need more. Sedatives are 40% weaker on them.

[b]Malignant hyperthermia[/b]
Some patients react to anesthetic [b]gas[/b]. Temperature climbs a degree every half minute. Over 42.5 they die.
• Stop using gas. Use propofol or ketamine instead.
• Give dantrolene. It takes 20 seconds to kick in.
Family history of "anesthesia complications" on the card means this.

[b]Order matters[/b]
Line first, sleep second, sanitize third. Sanitizing an awake patient is fine. Cutting one isn't.

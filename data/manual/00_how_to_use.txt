title: How to use this manual
Read it before you need it. You can't hold a scalpel and a book.

[b]Hands[/b]
You control one hand at a time. The mouse moves the active hand. Switch hands and the other one freezes exactly where it was, still doing what it was doing. A clamp stays clamped. A cautery pen left pressed keeps burning.

[b]Height[/b]
Hands rest just above whatever is under them: skin, tray, or what's inside an open wound. There is no height to set.
Hold [b]Lift[/b] to raise a hand over your partner's hands or tall tools. While a hand holds onto something, Lift pulls it up, slowly and steadily.
Hold [b]Crouch[/b] to get down to the floor. Anything that lands there is dirty: wash it at the sink, then sanitize it.

[b]Looking closer[/b]
The mouse wheel zooms. Zoomed in, the hand moves finer. The dot shows where the tool is working, and what your empty hand would pick up is highlighted.

[b]Using a tool[/b]
Hold [b]Use tool[/b] to press it onto the patient. The mouse wheel sets pressure while pressing: light, normal, deep. Deep goes through the skin.
Clamps and forceps toggle: press once to grab, press again to let go.

[b]Holding on[/b]
A hand that holds tissue stays put when you walk. Walk too far and you tear what you're holding. Let go first.

[b]Handing over[/b]
Hold a tool near your partner's empty hand and press [b]Grab[/b] to hand it over. Both hands still, or it hits the floor.

[b]Bumping[/b]
Your hands knock into your partner's hands. Hold [b]Lift[/b] to pass over them and over tall tools.

[b]Pages in this book[/b]
Basics first, then combinations. When a page doesn't match what you see on the monitor, trust the monitor.

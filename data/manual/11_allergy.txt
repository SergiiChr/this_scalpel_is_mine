title: 11. Drug allergy
## 11.1 Identification
The chart records only "known drug allergy". Order the allergy screen from the laboratory to identify the agent.

## 11.2 Alternatives
[table=3]
[cell][b]Allergy[/b][/cell][cell][b]Alternative[/b][/cell][cell][b]Caution[/b][/cell]
[cell]Cefazolin[/cell][cell]Clindamycin[/cell][cell]Longer delivery time[/cell]
[cell]Morphine[/cell][cell]Ketamine[/cell][cell]Raises heart rate[/cell]
[cell]Lidocaine[/cell][cell]General anesthesia, or topical cocaine[/cell][cell]Cocaine with adrenaline causes a cardiac event[/cell]
[cell]Propofol[/cell][cell]Ketamine or inhaled agent[/cell][cell]No inhaled agent with hyperthermia risk[/cell]
[/table]

## 11.3 Reactions
Urticaria and swelling: diphenhydramine.
Falling blood pressure and oxygen saturation indicate anaphylaxis: adrenaline immediately.

title: 7. Drug formulary
[b]7.1 Routes[/b]
• [b]Direct injection[/b] (syringe into the patient): rapid onset, high peak effect.
• [b]Intravenous infusion[/b] (at the infusion stand): slower, smoother onset. Requires a patent line (section 2). Blood products and crystalloids are given by this route only.

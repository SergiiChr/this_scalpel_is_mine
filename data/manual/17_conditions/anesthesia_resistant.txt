title: Intraoperative awareness
[i]The patient metabolizes anesthetic agents unusually fast.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Previous intraoperative awareness.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Anesthesia is not maintained and wears off within about {round(drug.propofol.Duration / quirk.anesthesia_resistant.anesthesia_decay_mult / 60)} minutes. The patient wakes talking, then screaming.

[b]Management[/b]
• Prepare a second dose before induction.
• Give it at the first sign of speech, or watch consciousness on the monitor.
> See Combined conditions (14.4).

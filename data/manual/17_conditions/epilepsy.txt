title: Epilepsy
[i]Recurrent seizures. Treatment was stopped.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “History of epileptic seizures.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Seizures come without warning. The table shakes, hands in contact are jolted, instruments slip and closures are strained.

[b]Management[/b]
• Give diazepam before any delicate step. It stops an active seizure and prevents new ones for five minutes.
> Low glucose seizes any patient, epileptic or not. See Combined conditions (14.9).

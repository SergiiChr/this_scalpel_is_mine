[b]Reaction[/b]
• The patient is going under, so only the monitor tells. Propofol alone lowers systolic pressure by {-drug.propofol.Peak.Bp} mmHg. With the allergy it falls by {Patient.AllergyPressureDrop} mmHg more at once and keeps falling.
• Pressure and oxygen saturation fall as on Drug allergy.

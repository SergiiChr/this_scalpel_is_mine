title: Lidocaine
[i]Allergy to the local anesthetic.[/i]

[b]Alternatives[/b]
[table=2]
[cell][b]Alternative[/b][/cell][cell][b]Caution[/b][/cell]
[cell]General anesthesia: propofol[/cell][cell]Needs an IV line. Lowers the pressure.[/cell]
[cell]General anesthesia: ketamine[/cell][cell]Raises heart rate and pressure.[/cell]
[cell]General anesthesia: inhaled agent[/cell][cell]Never with hyperthermia risk.[/cell]
[/table]

[b]Reaction[/b]
• Comes as the numbing injection goes in, while the patient is awake. The site still goes numb.
• Pressure and oxygen saturation fall as on Drug allergy.

title: Drug allergy
[i]A documented reaction to one of the standard agents.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Drug allergy reported, agent not documented.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• The allergen causes urticaria and swelling, a fall in blood pressure and possibly anaphylaxis.

[b]Management[/b]
• Order the allergy screen at the patient monitor ([b]Interact[/b]: Order blood work) before any drug is given. The result appears on the monitor when the laboratory reports.
• Until it does, give none of the agents below; where the patient cannot wait, use the alternatives.
• Use the alternative:

• Urticaria and swelling: diphenhydramine.
• Falling pressure and oxygen saturation mean anaphylaxis: adrenaline immediately.
• Never give the allergen again. Doses are in the drug table (7).

title: Coronary artery disease
[i]Narrowed coronary arteries. Little cardiac reserve.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Coronary artery disease.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Arrests {quirk.heart_weak.arrest_mult} times as readily from blood loss, pain and adverse drug combinations.

[b]Management[/b]
• Control bleeding early and keep pain covered.
• Keep the defibrillator close. Amiodarone improves shock success.

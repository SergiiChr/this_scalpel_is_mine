title: Situs inversus
[i]All viscera lie mirrored left to right.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] "Dextrocardia (situs inversus)."

[b]Hazards[/b]
• An incision planned by the standard approach lands on the wrong side.

[b]Management[/b]
• Plan the incision on the opposite side.
• Confirm the anatomy before marking.

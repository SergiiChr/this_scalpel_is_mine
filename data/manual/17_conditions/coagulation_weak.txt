title: Mild hemophilia
[i]Blood clots poorly.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Mild hemophilia.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Bleeds {(quirk.coagulation_weak.bleed_mult - 1) * 100}% faster than normal. Every nick matters.

[b]Management[/b]
• Give tranexamic acid (TXA) early.
• Never give heparin.
• Cauterize or clamp rather than pack.
> See Combined conditions (14.8).

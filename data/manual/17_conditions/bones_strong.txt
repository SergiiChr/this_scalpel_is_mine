title: High bone density
[i]Abnormally dense bone.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Unusually high bone density on previous X-ray.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• The standard bone saw does not cut it.
• Bones do not fracture by accident.

[b]Management[/b]
• Order the heavy saw from the nurse (about 45 seconds).
• Or use the mallet: it works on any bone but bruises the soft tissue around it.

title: Malignant hyperthermia
[i]An inherited reaction to volatile anesthetic agents.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Family history of anesthesia complications.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• The inhaled agent raises core temperature about one degree every thirty seconds. Fatal above 42.5 °C.

[b]Management[/b]
• Induce with propofol, or ketamine if propofol is unavailable. Never the inhaled agent.
• In a crisis: stop the inhaled agent and give dantrolene (onset about twenty seconds).
> See Combined conditions (14.5, 14.6).

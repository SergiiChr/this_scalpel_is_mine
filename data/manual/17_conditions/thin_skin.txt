title: Fragile skin
[i]Thin, aged skin with little elasticity.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Fragile skin, bruises easily.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Bruises {quirk.thin_skin.bruise_mult} times as easily.
• Tight clips cut through more often. Adhesive closures hold poorly.

[b]Management[/b]
• Retract gently.
• Close with sutures or staples.

title: Fragile skin
[i]Thin, aged skin with little elasticity.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] "Elderly, fragile skin."

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Tears at half the usual traction. Bruises twice as easily.
• Tight clips cut through twice as often. Adhesive closures hold poorly.

[b]Management[/b]
• Retract gently.
• Close with sutures or staples.

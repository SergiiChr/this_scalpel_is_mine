title: Anxiety disorder
[i]Severe anxiety in medical settings.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Anxiety disorder.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Panic builds twice as fast. A panicking patient flails and jolts the hands at the table.
• Panic raises blood pressure.

[b]Management[/b]
• Reassure from the head of the table, or sedate with diazepam.
> See Combined conditions (14.7).

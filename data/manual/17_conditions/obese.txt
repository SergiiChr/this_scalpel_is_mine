title: Obesity
[i]A thick subcutaneous fat layer.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “BMI 41.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Each blade pressure level reaches one layer shallower than expected.
• The wound is harder to hold open.

[b]Management[/b]
• Cut one level deeper than usual.
• Hold the wound with retractors rather than forceps.

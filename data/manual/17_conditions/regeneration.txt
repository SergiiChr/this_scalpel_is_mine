title: Abnormal tissue regeneration
[i]Tissue repairs itself far faster than normal. Not described in the literature.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Unusual tissue response noted in previous admission. Refused further tests.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Every wound closes on its own, incisions included.

[b]Management[/b]
• Work in short stages.
• Keep retractors in place while inside: held wounds close more slowly.
• Reopen a closed incision with the blade if the work is not finished.

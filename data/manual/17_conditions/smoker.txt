title: Heavy smoking
[i]Long-term tobacco use. Damaged airways.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Smoker, 40 pack-years.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Oxygen saturation runs about four points lower.
• The conscious patient has coughing fits that jolt the table and strain closures.

[b]Management[/b]
• Anesthesia stops the coughing.
• Do delicate work under general anesthesia where possible.

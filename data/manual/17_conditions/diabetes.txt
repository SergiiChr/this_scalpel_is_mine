title: Diabetes mellitus
[i]The patient makes no insulin of their own. Blood glucose drifts during surgery.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Type 1 diabetic, insulin dependent.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Glucose rises during surgery, faster under stress.
• Above {Patient.GLUCOSE_HIGH} mmol/l consciousness falls. Below {Patient.GLUCOSE_LOW} mmol/l the patient seizes.

[b]Management[/b]
• Check the level with the glucose panel ({Lab.PANELS.glucose.time} seconds).
• Insulin lowers it slowly and for a long time. Glucose D50 raises it quickly.
• A seizure from low glucose stops with diazepam, but returns until glucose is given.
> See Combined conditions (14.9).

title: Osteoporosis
[i]Thin, brittle bone.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] “Osteoporosis.”

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Any heavy impact fractures it, including a dropped instrument.
• The saw cuts {quirk.bones_fragile.saw_speed_mult} times as fast as usual.

[b]Management[/b]
• Keep heavy equipment, the defibrillator included, clear of the patient.
• Saw in short, light passes.

title: Thrombophilia
[i]Blood clots too readily.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] "Thrombophilia, prior DVT."

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Bleeds less than normal.
• A clot can form at any time and cause cardiac arrest without warning.

[b]Management[/b]
• Anticoagulate with heparin early, especially before a long procedure.
• Never give tranexamic acid.
> Heparin makes closures leak while it acts (5.5). See Combined conditions (14.8).

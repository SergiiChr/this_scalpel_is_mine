title: Implanted pacemaker
[i]A battery-driven device that keeps the heart to time. Older models are easily confused by stray current.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] "Implanted pacemaker."

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Electrocautery or defibrillation near the chest makes it misfire.
• Arrests half as often.

[b]Management[/b]
• On the chest, no electrocautery. Close with staples or sutures.
• No defibrillation unless the heart has arrested.

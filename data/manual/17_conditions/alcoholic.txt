title: Alcohol use disorder
[i]Chronic heavy alcohol use.[/i]

[b]On the chart:[/b] "Alcohol use disorder."

[b]Hazards[/b]
• Sedatives and anesthesia are about 40% less effective.
• Bleeds a little more than normal.

[b]Management[/b]
• Use higher doses and check anesthesia depth.
• Whiskey calms this patient without the usual side effects.
> With intraoperative awareness: give propofol through the IV line at the first sign of speech.

title: 18. Burns
## 18.1 Preparation
Burns are debrided and grafted under general anesthesia (section 8). Clean the burned skin with iodine first (1.5).

## 18.2 Debridement
Dead skin must be cut away before a graft will take. Draw the scalpel over the burned skin at level 1 (4.1), row by row, until it is cut away.

## 18.3 Grafting
Skin grafts are ordered from the nurse (Closing). Press the graft onto the debrided burn ([b]Use tool[/b]): each press places one patch, and a graft holds {tool.skin_graft.Charges} patches. Cover the debrided area patch by patch and order more as they run out.

## 18.4 Infection
Burned skin is an open wound. Give an antibiotic (7.3).

## 10.1 Group selection
Group O RhD-negative is the universal donor for red cells. Use it when the recipient's group is unknown.
> The chart may list a blood group. The laboratory confirms it.

## 10.2 Administration
Blood products are given only through an established intravenous line (section 2), at the infusion stand.

## 10.3 Incompatibility
An ABO-incompatible transfusion causes an acute hemolytic reaction: fever, swelling, hypotension and acute kidney injury. Stop the transfusion.

## 10.4 Bombay phenotype
Incompatible with all standard groups, including O-negative. Only Bombay-phenotype blood is compatible. Sourcing takes about two minutes: order it as soon as the phenotype appears on the chart.

## 10.5 Low blood volume
Patients with a low blood count start with a smaller reserve. Plan for less time.

title: 19. End of life
## 19.1 Assisted death
Only on the patient's documented request. Establish intravenous access (section 2), then give pentobarbital at the formulary dose (7.3) into the IV bag or a vein.

## 19.2 Presence
Stay with the patient until the end. Talk to them from the head of the table ([b]Interact[/b]): it eases what time they have left.

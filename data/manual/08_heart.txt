title: When the heart stops
[img=560]res://assets/manual/diagrams/rhythms.svg[/img]

[b]V-FIB[/b] (chaotic squiggle, alarm): shockable.
1. Clear the table. Anyone touching the patient during a shock gets shocked too.
2. Paddles on the chest. Hold to charge (2 s), release to shock.
3. Didn't take? Amiodarone, then shock again.
After 40 seconds of V-fib the heart goes flat.

[b]ASYSTOLE[/b] (flat line, constant tone): not shockable on its own.
1. Adrenaline first.
2. Then shock within the next minute.

[b]Never shock a beating heart.[/b] You can cause the arrest you were trying to fix.

[b]Pacemakers[/b] misfire near cautery and shocks. Use staples on the chest. Pacemaker patients arrest less.
[b]Weak hearts[/b] arrest from blood loss, pain and bad drug combos far sooner.

title: Advanced combinations
[b]Arteries[/b]: heparin [i]before[/i] the clamp. Clamp before you open. Close the vessel before you release.

[b]Stimulants[/b]: cocaine and adrenaline together cause a cardiac event. If the patient had cocaine, use atropine for a slow heart and amiodarone for rhythm.

[b]Pacemaker on the chest[/b]: no cautery, no shocks unless the heart has stopped. Staples and sutures only.

[b]Mirrored organs[/b]: plan the incision on the opposite side from the book. The card says "dextrocardia".

[b]Ticklish, awake[/b]: stay inside the site. Touching anywhere else makes them jump.

[b]Alcoholic + resistant to anesthesia[/b]: they wake up early and often. Give propofol through the IV at the first sign of talking.

[b]Strong clotting + long surgery[/b]: heparin early. A clot arrests them with no warning.

[b]Hyperthermia risk + no propofol[/b]: ketamine. Never the gas mask.

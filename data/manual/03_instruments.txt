title: 3. Instruments
[b]3.1 Sterility[/b]
Instruments on the tray are sterile until they touch the floor or a non-sterile surface. Personal items carried on the belt are not sterile.
An instrument that falls to the floor must be washed at the sink and then sanitized before it returns to the field.

[b]3.2 Standard set[/b]
Every procedure starts with the same tray: scalpel, forceps, kidney dish for removed items, cotton pads, iodine bottle with its dish, IV catheter and saline bag. Everything else is ordered from the nurse (3.4).

[b]3.3 Handling[/b]
Fine instruments cannot be used with large hands; heavy instruments cannot be used with small hands.

[b]3.4 Requests[/b]
Missing items are ordered from the nurse with the call bell: select the category, then the item. One request is handled at a time. Delivered items are placed on the delivery tray beside the bell. The board over the bell shows the item on its way, and after each delivery how long until the nurse takes the next request.

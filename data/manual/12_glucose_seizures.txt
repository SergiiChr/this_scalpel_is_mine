title: 12. Glucose and seizures
## 12.1 Diabetic patients
Blood glucose rises during surgery, faster under stress.
• Above 15 mmol/l: consciousness falls.
• Below 3 mmol/l: seizures.
Insulin acts slowly and for a long time; glucose acts quickly. Check levels with the glucose panel (fifteen seconds).

## 12.2 Seizures
A seizure shakes the table. Hands in contact with the patient are jolted, instruments slip and closures are strained.
Diazepam terminates an active seizure and prevents recurrence for five minutes.
> Patients with epilepsy seize without warning. Give diazepam before any delicate step.

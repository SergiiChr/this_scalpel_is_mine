title: 11. Bone
## 11.1 Osteotomy
Set the saw's speed with the wheel, hold it on the bone ([b]Use tool[/b]) and keep it there until the cut is complete. A saw applied to skin lacerates it.

## 11.2 Bone quality
The mallet is effective on any bone but bruises the surrounding soft tissue.
See Chronic conditions: High bone density, Osteoporosis.

## 11.3 Fracture reduction
Requires forceps on each fragment: with two operators in the room, one on each; operating alone, forceps in both hands.
1. Grasp each fragment with forceps.
2. Draw both back into alignment together.
3. Hold until reduction is confirmed.

## 11.4 Rib fractures
Reduce as above, forceps on each fragment. Free splinters are removed with slow, steady traction. They bleed on release: have suction ready.

## 11.5 Reduction rhinoplasty
The dorsal hump is bone superiorly and cartilage toward the tip, immediately beneath the skin.
Mark along the nasal dorsum, expose it, then reduce the hump with short, light strokes of the mallet. Remove the loose fragment with forceps. Close with fine sutures.

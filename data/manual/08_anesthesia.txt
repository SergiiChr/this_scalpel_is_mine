title: 8. Anesthesia management
## 8.1 Depth
Adequate depth is indicated by absent spontaneous respiration on the monitor. A patient who begins to speak is lightening.

## 8.2 Increased requirement
• Anesthetic resistance: agents are cleared at twice the usual rate. Prepare a second dose and give it before the patient surfaces.
• Chronic alcohol use: higher doses are required; sedatives are about 40% less effective.

## 8.3 Malignant hyperthermia
A pharmacogenetic reaction to volatile anesthetic agents. Core temperature rises about one degree every thirty seconds and is fatal above 42.5 °C.
1. Discontinue the inhaled agent. Continue with propofol or ketamine.
2. Give dantrolene. Onset about twenty seconds.
> A family history of "anesthesia complications" on the chart indicates susceptibility.

## 8.4 Sequence
IV access, then induction, then skin preparation. Skin preparation may be done in the conscious patient; incision may not.

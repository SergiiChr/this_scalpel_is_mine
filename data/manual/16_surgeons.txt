title: Looking after yourselves
[b]Stress[/b] rises with every mistake, alarm and scream. Full stress: you pass out for a few seconds and drop what you hold. It drains while things are calm.

[b]Sickness[/b] rises from blood (for some), bad breath up close and your partner's vomit. Full: you vomit. Aim away from the patient.

[b]Shaky hands[/b]: hold your breath to steady. The breath meter runs out.

[b]Sweat[/b]: slippery gloves. Change them at the glove box. A cap stops the drip into open wounds.

[b]Bumps[/b]: two hands in the same spot collide. Lift yours over, or agree on sides.

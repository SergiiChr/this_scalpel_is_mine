title: Before you cut
[img=520]res://assets/manual/diagrams/setup_order.svg[/img]

[b]1. IV line.[/b] Use the IV catheter on an arm. Without a line, anything you give through the IV stand does nothing.

[b]2. Sanitize.[/b] Paint the site with the iodine swab until it's orange-brown. Unsanitized skin means infection later.

[b]3. Numb or sleep.[/b]
• [b]General:[/b] propofol (IV) or anesthetic gas mask. Wait for the monitor to show no respiration count, then start.
• [b]Local:[/b] lidocaine at the site. The patient stays awake and talks. Keep them calm.
• [b]None:[/b] some scenarios. Work fast, talk to them.

[b]4. Mark.[/b] Draw the line before you cut it. Marked incisions score better and keep you honest.

[b]Read the chart[/b], but on some nights the records get mixed up. If the lobby warned you about a chart mix-up, don't trust the blood type or allergies until the nurse brings the corrected copy.

Awake patients panic. Panicking patients thrash. Talk to them at the head of the bed, or sedate with diazepam.

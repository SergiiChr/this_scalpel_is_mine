[b]15.1 Repositioning[/b]
Supine, lateral, prone: one step at a time, never directly from supine to prone.
1. All staff at the table stand on the same side.
2. Release all tissue and instruments. No one holds the patient's tissue during a turn.
3. Follow the prompts together as they appear. A mistimed movement rolls the patient off the table.

[b]15.2 Falls[/b]
A fall from the table can fracture the hip, disrupt closures and cause further blood loss. It must be reported.

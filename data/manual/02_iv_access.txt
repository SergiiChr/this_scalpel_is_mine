title: 2. Intravenous access and infusion
## 2.1 Indications
Required before any intravenous drug, fluid or blood product. Establish the line before induction: once the patient is under, access is harder and every minute counts.

## 2.2 Equipment
• IV catheter (from the tray, or order it from the nurse: Blood and fluids).
• Infusion stand with giving set, positioned beside the table.
• The drug, fluid bag or blood pack to be given.

## 2.3 Cannulation
1. Choose a site on the forearm or the back of the hand, away from the operative field. Prefer the arm on the side of the infusion stand, so the tubing does not cross the walkway.
2. Take the catheter in the working hand and connect the giving set to it at the infusion stand ([b]Connect the IV line[/b]).
3. Rest the tip on the skin over the vein and lower it ([b]Use tool[/b]) until the catheter seats. Hold it steady; a moving hand misses the vein. Zoom all the way in for a close view of the needle over the vein.
> A catheter that misses the vein, or goes in without the giving set connected, still sticks, but nothing will run through it and it cannot be connected afterwards. Seat a new catheter with the giving set connected in place of an unconnected one; a connected catheter outside the vein only comes out with the tubing (2.5).
4. The tubing runs from the stand to the site. The catheter is single use.
> A catheter dropped on the floor is no longer sterile. Take the giving set off it at the infusion stand ([b]Disconnect the IV line[/b]), wash and sanitize it, then connect it again.

## 2.4 Administering an infusion
1. Drugs: rest the syringe needle on the bag hanging on the infusion stand and push the plunger. The dose starts down the line at once, about {ToolActions.DripRate} ml a second, and acts as it runs in. Drawing back takes fluid from the bag.
2. Fluid bags and blood packs: hold the new bag at the infusion stand and select [b]Swap IV bag[/b]. It replaces the hanging bag and runs in.
3. Onset is slower and smoother than direct injection. Fluids and blood products are given by this route only.
> Without a patent line, a drug pushed into the bag stays there and a swapped bag is wasted.

## 2.5 Line security
The tubing hangs loose between stand and patient. Brushing it only pushes it aside, but tubing caught on the legs or hands and walked away with tears the catheter out of the arm: the skin rips, the line is lost and must be re-sited with a new catheter. Step around the tubing, or free it before walking off.

## 2.6 Fluid choice
• Crystalloid (saline, {drug.saline.Peak.VolumeMl} ml): restores circulating volume after moderate blood loss.
• Blood products: see section 10. Confirm the group before connecting.

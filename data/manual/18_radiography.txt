title: 18. Intraoperative radiography
[b]18.1 Indications[/b]
Localization of radiopaque foreign bodies (bullets, blades, retained instruments), assessment of bone, and a count check before closure.

[b]18.2 Procedure[/b]
1. Take hold of the mobile unit's handle ([b]Interact[/b]). It follows until released.
2. Position it beside the table.
3. Expose. All staff stand clear during the exposure, which lasts a few seconds.
4. The film is delivered onto the cart. It develops over about six seconds.
The unit requires about thirty seconds between exposures.

[b]18.3 Interpretation[/b]
[table=2]
[cell][b]Appearance[/b][/cell][cell][b]Finding[/b][/cell]
[cell]Bright white[/cell][cell]Metal: projectiles, blades, instruments[/cell]
[cell]Light grey, linear[/cell][cell]Bone[/cell]
[cell]Soft grey, rounded[/cell][cell]Soft tissue mass, organ, clot[/cell]
[cell]Black[/cell][cell]Trapped air[/cell]
[/table]

[b]18.4 Before closure[/b]
Take a film. A retained instrument will be found on the next film regardless of who takes it.

title: Closing
[img=560]res://assets/manual/diagrams/closures.svg[/img]

A wound is only closed when you've gone along [b]all[/b] of it. Move the tool along the line while pressing.

[table=3]
[cell][b]Method[/b][/cell][cell][b]Speed[/b][/cell][cell][b]Holds[/b][/cell]

Seizures, coughing, falls and bumps strain closures. Weak closures burst open. Internal wounds (stomach, bowel, arteries) close with sutures or cautery inside the cavity.

title: Blood sugar and seizures
[b]Diabetics[/b] drift upward during surgery. Stress makes it faster.
• Above 15: they fade out, consciousness drops.
• Below 3: they seize.
Insulin takes a while and lasts a while. Glucose hits fast. Check with the glucose panel (15 s).

[b]Seizures[/b] shake the whole table. Hands in contact get jolted, tools slip, closures strain.
Diazepam stops an active seizure and prevents new ones for 5 minutes.
Epileptic patients seize at random. Give diazepam [b]before[/b] the delicate part.
